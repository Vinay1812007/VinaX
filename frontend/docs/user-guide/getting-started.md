# Make VinaX yours

Choose a name, a username and your listening languages on the welcome screen. The four-step tour introduces Home, playback, AI and your library. You can skip it after setup and return to guided walkthroughs from Help.

## Start with one song

Press **Play my mix** on Home, or search for an artist you love. Save a favourite with the heart. The Home checklist updates as you play, save and try the walkthrough; established listeners do not need to keep completing onboarding tasks.

## Your queue

Albums and playlists play their selected songs in order. Use Play next or Add to queue in a song menu, then reorder or remove songs on the Queue page. Playback stops at the end unless repeat is enabled.

Home displays catalog shelves; its layout is managed by the app. Taste Profile and Settings still control your listening preferences.

## Turn a moment into a playlist

Open **AI Playlist**. Choose a starting point such as Deep focus or After hours; the suggestion uses your first selected language. Edit the text, then select **Build my playlist**. Once songs arrive, choose Play or Save.

If the service is unavailable, your idea remains in the text box so you can try again. Creating a playlist does not start playback automatically.

## Keep your music safe

Your saved library and settings live on this device. Export a backup from **Settings → Your Data** before clearing browser data or reinstalling. AI features send relevant context to the service; optional usage sharing can be changed in Settings.

See [Import & Export Your Data](import-export-your-data.md) for backup and transfer instructions.
