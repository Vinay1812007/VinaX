# Legal and copyright notes

This document records the legal position the product is built around: what kind of service VinaX is, who owns the content it plays, how takedowns work, how listener data is treated, how it is paid for and which kinds of outside services it depends on. It is a plain-language summary for maintainers, not legal advice. The listener-facing pages are `/terms`, `/privacy`, `/dmca` and `/contact` in the app.

| Topic | Position |
| --- | --- |
| Nature of the service | VinaX is a free music player with no sign-up. It streams music, artwork and lyrics from public catalogue sources. It hosts no media files, charges nothing and sells no data. |
| Copyright | All songs, sound recordings, compositions, artwork and lyrics remain the property of their artists, composers, labels and publishers. VinaX shows attribution (title, artists, album, film) as provided by the catalogue source. |
| Takedowns | Rights holders can ask for removal through the in-app DMCA page (`/dmca`) or the contact page (`/contact`). A verified request is honoured by adding the content to the server-side blocklist (`/api/blocklist`), which every client reads. |
| Advertising | The website shows one labelled advertisement at the end of browsing pages (song, album, artist, language and mood pages). There is none in the player, the queue, the Android app or Kid mode; on those surfaces the advertising script is never loaded (`frontend/src/components/AdSlot.tsx`). Where it does load, the advertising network receives the request directly from the browser. |
| Listener data | There are no accounts. Favourites, history and the taste profile are stored on the listener's device, with export and erase. Server-side usage statistics depend on the listener's usage-sharing choice. Exactly what is stored and what leaves the device is in [data-and-privacy.md](data-and-privacy.md); that document is the authority, and this row must not say more than it does. |
| Outside services, by kind | A public music catalogue and its media CDN; an open lyrics database; an advertising network on the pages named above; static hosting and an edge Worker platform; a push service for notifications; a hosted database that holds usage rows, published configuration, push tokens and short-lived rooms; AI model providers for VinaX AI and the music AI features, reached only through the Worker with the summaries listed in [data-and-privacy.md](data-and-privacy.md#ai). The operational names are in [operations.md](operations.md) and `backend/.env.example`. |
| AI generated songs | "Generate a song like this" makes new, original tracks with Gemini's music models, on the key VinaX AI already uses. The model receives only a description of a feel (mood, energy, tempo, genre, language, length) and the listener's own words; never another song's audio, title, artist or lyrics. Every request tells it to compose an entirely new piece, without naming, imitating or referencing any real artist or work and without reproducing existing melodies or lyrics, and a listener's words that name a real artist or song are refused. Every generated track is labelled "AI generated" on cards, in Now Playing, on the lock screen and on share cards, and it never appears in charts or trend surfaces. Kept on the server for 24 hours; a listener who saves one keeps it on their device. The model provider's terms decide what may be done with its output, and its own safety filter refuses prompts for specific artist voices or copyrighted lyrics; every track carries its inaudible watermark marking it as AI-made. The owner must check those terms before switching generation on. |
| Disclaimer | The service is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. Catalogue availability depends on outside sources and can change without notice. |
