# Legal and copyright notes

This document records the legal position the product is built around: what kind of service VinaX is, who owns the content it plays, how takedowns work, how listener data is treated, how it is paid for and which kinds of outside services it depends on. It is a plain-language summary for maintainers, not legal advice. The listener-facing pages are `/terms`, `/privacy`, `/dmca` and `/contact` in the app.

| Topic | Position |
| --- | --- |
| Nature of the service | VinaX is a free music player with no sign-up. It streams music, artwork and lyrics from public catalogue sources. It hosts no media files, charges nothing and sells no data. |
| Copyright | All songs, sound recordings, compositions, artwork and lyrics remain the property of their artists, composers, labels and publishers. VinaX shows attribution (title, artists, album, film) as provided by the catalogue source. |
| Takedowns | Rights holders can ask for removal through the in-app DMCA page (`/dmca`) or the contact page (`/contact`). A verified request is honoured by adding the content to the server-side blocklist (`/api/blocklist`), which every client reads. |
| Advertising | The website shows one labelled advertisement at the end of browsing pages (song, album, artist, language and mood pages). There is none in the player, the queue, VinaX AI, the Android app or Kid mode; on those surfaces the advertising script is never loaded (`frontend/src/components/AdSlot.tsx`). Where it does load, the advertising network receives the request directly from the browser. |
| Listener data | There are no accounts. Favourites, history and the taste profile are stored on the listener's device, with export and erase. Server-side usage statistics depend on the listener's usage-sharing choice. Exactly what is stored and what leaves the device is in [data-and-privacy.md](data-and-privacy.md); that document is the authority, and this row must not say more than it does. |
| Outside services, by kind | A public music catalogue and its media CDN; an open lyrics database; hosted AI model endpoints reached with server-side keys; an advertising network on the pages named above; static hosting and an edge Worker platform; a push service for notifications; a hosted database that holds usage rows, published configuration, push tokens and short-lived rooms. The operational names are in [operations.md](operations.md) and `backend/.env.example`. |
| Disclaimer | The service is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. Catalogue availability depends on outside sources and can change without notice. |
