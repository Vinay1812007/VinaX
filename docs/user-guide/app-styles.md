# App styles

This page covers app styles: what one is, the six you can choose from, how to switch, what a style does and does not change, and how styles work together with the theme, the accent colour, festival themes and reduced motion. It applies to the web app and the Android app.

## What an app style is

An app style is a complete look for VinaX. One choice changes the colours, the type, the shapes of cards and buttons, how the navigation and the mini player are laid out, the backdrop of the full-screen player and how things move.

A style does not change your music. Your library, queue, history, recommendations and every other setting stay exactly as they are, and every control keeps its name and its job. You can switch as often as you like.

## The six styles

| Style | In one line |
|---|---|
| **Aura** | Clean glass and large titles |
| **Pulse** | Dense, flat and fast |
| **Sangam** | Warm, roomy and colourful |
| **Nocturne** | Midnight gradients and glow |
| **Marquee** | Black canvas, picture first |
| **Vibe** | Immersive, bubbly and bold |

**Aura** is the style VinaX starts with. It is cool graphite with an iris-blue accent. The bars are frosted glass, pages open with a large left-aligned title, artwork has soft rounded corners and the play button is a circle. Choose it if you want a calm, uncluttered screen.

**Pulse** is neutral black with a bright yellow-green accent. Nothing is glass: every bar is a solid panel, corners are tight, and rows and tiles are compact, so more fits on a screen. The top of a page takes a wash of the playing song's artwork colour, and the player is a solid bar across the bottom. Motion is short. Choose it if you want density and speed.

**Sangam** is plum and marigold with a teal second colour. Artwork and cards are big and rounded, tiles are washed in colour, and the sidebar and the phone tab bar float as rounded panels. Motion is gentle. Choose it if you like a warm, generous layout. It is also the look VinaX had before app styles existed.

**Nocturne** is deep indigo with an orchid accent. The page sits on a slow gradient, titles carry an orchid-to-aqua gradient, play buttons glow and tabs are marked with an underline. Motion is slow. Choose it for late-night listening or if you like a little glow.

**Marquee** is true black with an ice-blue accent. Artwork is large and square-cornered, type is plain, thin lines replace panels, the play button is white, and the player bar spans the screen with its progress drawn along the top edge. There is no glass and almost no motion. Choose it if you want the pictures to lead and the app to stay out of the way.

**Vibe** is deep teal with a coral accent. Shapes are as round as they go: bubble artwork, a capsule player and a floating capsule tab bar. On a phone the tab bar shows icons without their names, and the tab you are on is a coloured bubble. Type is oversized and things spring a little when pressed. Choose it if you want the app to feel playful.

## Switch style

1. Open **Settings → Appearance → App style**.
2. Choose one of the six cards. Each card is a small drawing of the style with its name and one line about it.
3. The app changes at once. The line under the cards names the style you chose and what it changes.

On a keyboard, the cards are one group: move between them with the arrow keys.

The welcome also offers the same choice as its **Pick your look** step, and Help → **Replay the welcome tour** shows that step again. Help has a guided tutorial, **Change the app's look**, that walks through it on the real screen.

## Styles and the theme

**Settings → Appearance → Theme** still chooses between Dark, Black, Light, System and Auto (light by day, dark at night). Every style has its own dark look and its own light look. Black is true black in every style, which suits screens that switch dark pixels off. In Light, the bars, sheets and cards are frosted glass over three soft lights — the style's colour, its second colour and the artwork of the song playing — so the look changes gently with your music.

## Styles and the accent colour

**Settings → Appearance → Accent color** sets the colour of play buttons, progress and the things you have selected.

- **Style colour**, the first swatch, is the style's own accent. While it is selected, the accent changes whenever you change style.
- **Marigold**, Copper, Sunset, Gold, Emerald, Ocean, Azure, Violet, Rose and Mono replace the style's accent. A colour you choose here stays when you change style.
- **Custom accent** takes any colour; VinaX builds a readable palette from it. **Use a preset** goes back to the swatches.
- **Dynamic theme** takes the accent from the playing song's artwork instead.

## Styles and festival themes

On a festival day the festival's colours replace the style's colours. The layout of your style stays: the same navigation, the same player, the same shapes. When the festival ends, your style's own colours return. See [Festival themes](festival-themes.md).

## Reduced motion

When you change style, the app cross-fades to the new look where the browser supports it. With **Settings → Appearance → Reduce motion** on, or when your device asks for less motion, the change is immediate, and each style's own movement is reduced too.

## Where the choice is kept

Your style is saved on this device with your other settings, and it is included in backups. See [Library and backup](library-and-backup.md).

## If something looks wrong

| What you see | Why, and what to do |
|---|---|
| The colours look different today | A festival theme is on. It ends with the festival. To switch it off, see [Festival themes](festival-themes.md#switch-festival-themes-off). |
| I want the look VinaX used to have | Choose **Sangam**. If you also want its marigold accent in another style, choose the **Marigold** swatch. |
| The accent did not change when I changed style | You have chosen an accent of your own. Choose **Style colour**, the first swatch. If a custom colour is set, press **Use a preset** first. |
| The bars are not see-through | Pulse and Marquee have no glass. In the other styles, check **Glass effect** and **Background blur** in Settings → Appearance; the Black theme keeps bars solid. |
| The accent keeps changing with each song | **Dynamic theme** is on. Turn it off in Settings → Appearance. |
