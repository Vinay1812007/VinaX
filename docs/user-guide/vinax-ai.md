# VinaX AI

This page covers VinaX AI, the assistant you can chat with inside VinaX: opening it, asking and steering replies, web search, adding files, the side panel for code, pages and diagrams, talking to it by voice, keeping and deleting your chats, and what to do when it is busy. VinaX AI is optional, and the chat never sees your listening. The music AI features (an AI-built Home, and the AI's order for Up Next) are separate: see [Getting started](getting-started.md#a-home-built-for-you) and [The player and the queue](player-and-queue.md#ai-fine-tunes-up-next).

## Open VinaX AI

VinaX AI needs you to be signed in with Google, so your chats can follow you to every device. If you are not signed in, the page asks you to sign in first.

- On a computer, choose **VinaX AI** in the side menu.
- On a phone, open your profile picture and choose **VinaX AI**.
- Anywhere, press `Ctrl`+`K` (`⌘`+`K` on a Mac) and type "VinaX AI".

Your music keeps playing. The small player at the top of the chat shows the song, and tapping it pauses or plays. **Back to VinaX** returns you to the app.

## Ask something

1. Type in the box at the bottom. `Enter` sends; `Shift`+`Enter` starts a new line.
2. The reply appears as it is written. **Stop** (the square) ends it early.
3. A new chat offers four starters, **Write**, **Learn**, **Code** and **Music**. Each fills in the start of a request for you to finish. Nothing is sent until you send it.

When VinaX AI thinks before answering, a **Thinking…** line shows above the reply. It folds into **Thought for N s** when the answer starts; open it to read the reasoning.

Under each reply:
- **Copy** copies the reply.
- **Retry** asks again. Its arrow lets you retry with **Auto** or with another model.
- **Read aloud** reads the reply to you (see [Voice](#talk-to-vinax-ai-by-voice)).
- If a reply was cut short, **Continue** carries on from where it stopped.
- The name under a reply says which model wrote it.

To change a question you asked, hover over it (or tap it on a phone) and choose **Edit**. Your edit is sent as a new version and gets its own answer. The old version stays: use **‹ 2 / 3 ›** beside a message to move between versions.

## Choose how it answers

The menu beside the send button picks the model.
- **Auto** is the default and the best choice for most questions. It picks a quick model for everyday questions, one that can see for images, one with a long memory for long documents and long chats, and a stronger one for hard problems. If one is busy, Auto moves to another.
- Every model is also listed by group, with a one-line description and badges for what it can do: see images, search the web, think before answering, and write code. A greyed-out model cannot answer right now, and says why.
- The list is long, so it has a search box: type part of a name, a provider or what you need (for example "translate" or "code"), then press Enter to pick the first match.
- **More models** holds specialist models, such as a translator and an Arabic and English model. Models marked "slow to start" can take up to a minute before the first word appears.
- A model you pick yourself is never swapped for another. If it is busy, the reply offers **Retry**, **Try with Auto** and other models to try.

The **+** button holds more choices:
- **Extended thinking** makes VinaX AI think longer before answering hard questions. The reply takes longer.
- **Web search**: **Auto** searches when you ask about something current, such as news, prices, scores or the weather. **On** searches for every message, and **Off** never searches.
- **Add photos and files**, described below, and **Settings**.

When a reply used the web, small numbered chips in the text link to its sources, and a **Sources** row under the reply lists them. While it searches, you see what it is looking for.

## Add photos and files

Add files from **+** → **Add photos and files**, by pasting, or by dropping them on the page. They wait above the message box until you send; **×** removes one.

| Kind | Up to |
|---|---|
| Photos (PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF) | 10 per message. Large photos are shrunk on your device before they are sent |
| PDFs | 30 MB and 500 pages each |
| Text, Markdown, CSV, JSON and code files | 2 MB each |

A message can carry 20 files, and a chat 50. These are the usual limits; VinaX's owner can change them, and VinaX AI tells you the current one if you go over. If the model you picked cannot see images, a note offers one that can, or Auto, before you send.

## Make it yours

The gear at the top of VinaX AI opens its settings.
- **General**: the theme (the same as the rest of VinaX), whether VinaX AI makes artifacts (below), and whether pages, charts and diagrams show as live previews right in the reply.
- **Personalisation**: what VinaX AI should call you, what it should know about you (your work, interests, what you're learning), how it should reply (tone, length, format, language), and a reply style: Normal, Concise, Explanatory, Formal or Learning (it guides you to work things out). These stay on your device and go with each message; they are not saved with your chats.

## Code, pages, charts and diagrams

When VinaX AI writes something substantial, such as a web page, a small app, a chart, a diagram, a picture, a document or a file of code, it appears as a card in the reply and opens in a panel beside the chat. On a phone the panel is full screen. When a picture explains something better than words, such as numbers to compare or steps in a process, VinaX AI draws a chart or diagram by itself. Pages, charts and diagrams also show as a live preview under the card, right in the reply. If one can't be drawn, the preview says so and offers **Fix it**, which asks VinaX AI for a corrected version.

- **Preview** shows web pages, small apps, pictures and diagrams working. They run in a separate, locked-down space, so nothing in them can reach your account or the app.
- **Code** shows the source.
- **Copy** and **Download** save what is shown.
- When VinaX AI updates something it made, the panel shows **v2 of 2**. Use **‹ ›** to step between versions.
- On a computer, drag the panel's edge to make it wider or narrower. **Close** returns to the chat.

### Everything VinaX AI made, in one place

**Artifacts** in the sidebar (just under **New chat**), or **Settings** → **Your chats** → **Your artifacts**, opens a page with every page, app, chart, diagram, picture, document and piece of code your chats made, newest first.

- Each card shows its name, what kind it is (with the programming language for code), how many versions there are, and the chat and day it came from.
- The buttons along the top (**Pages**, **Components**, **Diagrams**, **Images**, **Documents**, **Code**) show one kind at a time, and the search box finds things by name. Scroll down for older ones.
- Tap a card to open it in the same panel as in a chat, with **Preview**, **Code**, **Copy**, **Download** and **‹ ›** for its versions.
- **Open chat** takes you to the reply that made the version you're looking at, with it open beside the chat.
- Nothing extra is saved for this page: it shows what's already in your chats. Delete a chat and its artifacts leave this page too.

## Talk to VinaX AI by voice

Voice controls appear when the service has voice switched on.

- **Dictate**: tap the microphone in the message box and speak. Tap **✓** when you finish, or **×** to throw the recording away. Your words appear in the message box for you to check and fix. They are never sent by themselves. Recordings can be up to two minutes.
- **Read aloud**: tap the speaker under a reply. Code, links and source numbers are skipped, and anything in the side panel is named rather than read. Tap it again to stop.
- **Live voice**: with nothing typed in the message box, tap the sound-wave button where Send usually is. A full-screen view opens and you can simply talk, with captions of what each of you says. Talking over VinaX AI stops it so you can carry on. The microphone button mutes you, and **End** (or `Esc`) closes it. What you both said is saved into the chat as ordinary messages. A Live voice session lasts about ten minutes; start another to keep talking.
- **Web search in Live voice**: when web search is available, Live voice can look things up as you talk (news, tour dates, charts, anything current). It says it's checking, answers out loud and tells you which site it used; the sites appear under the captions and are saved with the reply. The globe button in Live voice turns it on or off (Live voice reconnects for a moment and carries on where you were), and **Settings** → **Search the web in Live voice** sets it for next time.
- **Settings** (the gear at the top, **Settings** in the sidebar, or **+** → **Settings**) has VinaX AI's own settings: the theme, Personalisation, artifacts and live previews, the model, Extended thinking, web search, your **microphone** (with **Test microphone**, which shows a level bar and says whether it hears you), the voice that reads replies, the Live voice (it also reads long replies), the Live voice model, **Search the web in Live voice**, **Export all chats**, **Your artifacts** and your **Shared links**. They are kept on this device, apart from the theme (VinaX's own) and the shared links, which live with your account.

Your music pauses while the microphone is open and plays on quietly while a reply is read, then comes back as it was. The first time, your browser or phone asks to use the microphone. In the Android app it is the same question voice search asks.

## Your chats

- The sidebar lists your chats: **Starred** first, then by day. On a phone, open it with **☰**.
- Search finds chats by their title and by what was said in them.
- Each chat's **⋯** menu can **Star**, **Rename**, **Move to project…** or **Delete** it. A deleted chat disappears at once and is removed for good within a day.
- Chats are kept with your Google account, so they follow you to every device you sign in on. Each chat has its own address you can bookmark; only you can open it.
- **Settings** → **Your data** → **Export VinaX AI chats** downloads every chat, with all its versions, and your projects, as one file.
- Deleting your account deletes every chat, project and file with it.

## Projects

A project keeps related chats together, with instructions and files that every chat in it uses: a course you're studying, a band you're writing for, a trip you're planning.

- Press **Projects** in the sidebar (on a phone, open it with **☰**), then **New project**. Give it a name and, if you like, a short description.
- **Instructions**: press **Add** and tell VinaX AI how to work in this project, such as a role, a tone, a format or what to focus on. Every chat in the project follows them, unless you ask for something different in the chat. Up to 8,000 characters.
- **Files**: press **Add files** to give the project text, code, PDFs or images. Every chat in the project can read them. A project holds up to 20 files and 200,000 characters of text. The bin button removes one.
- **Start a chat in the project** with the message box on the project's page. The chat opens with the project's name at the top; tap it to go back to the project.
- **Move a chat** into a project, or out of one, from its **⋯** menu → **Move to project…**.
- The project's page lists its chats. The star, pencil and bin at the top star, rename or delete the project.
- **Delete project** deletes its instructions and files. Its chats stay as ordinary chats, unless you choose **Delete them too**.
- A shared link to a chat in a project never shows the project's instructions or files.

## Share a chat

- Open a chat and press **Share** (the arrow at the top, next to the gear), then **Create link**. Anyone you give the link to can read the chat, even without a VinaX account.
- The link shows the chat as it is when you make it: your messages and VinaX AI's replies, with any code, pages or diagrams. Files you added show by name only, never their contents. VinaX AI's reasoning and your name aren't shown.
- Messages you send afterwards aren't added. To show them, open **Share** again and press **Update link**; the link stays the same.
- **Copy link** puts it on your clipboard; **Share…** (on phones) opens your device's share sheet.
- **Stop sharing** deletes the copy and the link stops working. Deleting the chat or your account does the same.
- **Settings** → **Your chats** → **Shared links** lists every chat you've shared, with **Copy** and **Stop sharing** for each.
- Someone signed in who opens your link can press **Continue this chat** to carry on in a new chat of their own. Your chat isn't changed.

## When something goes wrong

| You see | What it means |
|---|---|
| A model "is busy right now" | That model has reached its limit for now. Use **Try with Auto**, another model from the reply, or **Retry** in a minute |
| "VinaX AI is taking a break" | VinaX AI has been switched off for a while. Your music keeps playing |
| "VinaX AI is unavailable right now" | The service cannot reach any AI provider. Try again later |
| "Voice is busy right now" | Voice has reached its limit for now. Try again in a minute, or type |
| "Preview needs the sandbox host" | Previews are not available at the moment. **Code**, **Copy** and **Download** still work |
| The microphone does not start | Allow the microphone for VinaX in your browser's site settings, or in the phone's app settings. In the Android app, update VinaX if it offers an update |
| You speak but nothing moves, or "No sound is reaching VinaX AI" | Your computer is sending VinaX AI a silent microphone (often a virtual one such as BlackHole). Open **Settings** → **Microphone**, pick your real mic and press **Test microphone**. In Live voice, tap the microphone's name to switch |

VinaX AI can make mistakes, so check anything important. What you send is passed to an AI provider to write the reply; the [privacy page](https://www.vinaxmusic.online/privacy) says which providers and what is kept.
