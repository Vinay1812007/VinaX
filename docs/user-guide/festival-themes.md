# Festival themes

This page covers what VinaX does on festival days: the look, the greeting card and the strip on Home, how to preview any festival, how to switch festival themes off, what Data saver and reduced motion change, and the list of festivals. It applies to the web app and the Android app.

## What happens on a festival day

- **The look.** The app takes the festival's colours: its accent, a tinted background, a ribbon along the top and a soft backdrop with a glow, a faint pattern, a large emblem and a few slow particles. The look starts the day before the festival and ends with it. The layout of your [app style](app-styles.md) stays as it is; only the colours change.
- **The greeting card.** The first time you open VinaX during a festival, a card shows the festival's emblem, its greeting and one line about it. **Play … songs** opens a search for that festival's music. **Continue** closes the card, and so does `Esc`. You see the card once per festival per year, and never on top of the welcome or the What's new sheet.
- **The Home strip.** While a festival is on, Home shows a strip with the greeting and **Play festive songs**. The × on the strip hides it.

On Independence Day and Republic Day the backdrop is a waving flag instead of an emblem.

## Preview a festival

1. Open **Settings → Appearance → Preview a festival**.
2. Choose a festival from the list. Its look applies at once and its greeting card is shown.
3. To end it, choose **Stop preview** in the same list, or reload the app.

A preview lasts only until you stop it or reload. It works whether festival themes are on or off, and it does not count as having seen that festival's greeting card. Help has a guided tutorial, **Festival themes**, that walks through this.

## Switch festival themes off

Open **Settings → Appearance** and turn **Festival themes** off. With it off there is no festival look, no greeting card and no Home strip. The line under the switch tells you which festival is on now, or which one is next and in how many days.

## Data saver and reduced motion

With **Data saver** on, with **Reduce motion** on, or when your device asks for less motion, nothing in the festival backdrop moves: the particles and the waving flag are not shown. The colours and the greeting stay.

The particles are also few by design, fewer on a phone than on a computer, and they pause while the app is in the background.

## The festivals

VinaX has 43 festival looks. Forty switch on by themselves on these dates. Dates marked 2026 follow the lunar calendar or fall on a different day each year, and are the dates for 2026.

| Month | Festivals |
|---|---|
| January | Sankranti · Pongal · Lohri (Jan 13–16); Republic Day (Jan 25–26) |
| February | Valentine's Day (Feb 13–14); Maha Shivaratri (Feb 15, 2026) |
| March | Holi (Mar 3–4, 2026); Women's Day (Mar 8); Ugadi · Gudi Padwa (Mar 18–19, 2026); Eid ul-Fitr (Mar 20–21, 2026); Sri Rama Navami (Mar 26–27, 2026) |
| April | Easter (Apr 3–5, 2026); Vishu · Baisakhi · Puthandu · Bihu (Apr 14); Akshaya Tritiya (Apr 19, 2026) |
| May | Buddha Purnima (May 1, 2026); Mother's Day (May 10, 2026); Hanuman Jayanti (May 12–13, 2026); Eid ul-Adha (Bakrid) (May 27, 2026) |
| June | Telangana Formation Day (Jun 2); Father's Day (Jun 21, 2026) |
| July | Bonalu (Jul 12–26, 2026); Guru Purnima (Jul 29, 2026) |
| August | Friendship Day (Aug 2, 2026); Independence Day (Aug 14–15); Varalakshmi Vratam (Aug 21, 2026); Onam (Aug 25–27, 2026); Raksha Bandhan (Aug 28, 2026) |
| September | Krishna Janmashtami (Sep 3–4, 2026); Teachers' Day (Sep 5); Ganesh Chaturthi (Sep 13–15, 2026) |
| October | Gandhi Jayanti (Oct 2); Bathukamma (Oct 10–18, 2026); Dussehra (Oct 19–20, 2026); Halloween (Oct 31) |
| November | Andhra Pradesh Formation Day (Nov 1); Diwali, Dhanteras to Bhai Dooj (Nov 6–10, 2026); Nagula Chavithi (Nov 13–14, 2026); Chhath Puja (Nov 15–16, 2026); Guru Nanak Jayanti (Nov 24, 2026) |
| December | Vaikunta Ekadasi (Dec 19–20, 2026); Christmas (Dec 24–25); New Year (Dec 31 – Jan 1) |

Three more looks are in the preview list but do not switch on by themselves, because they overlap another festival or last a whole month: Navratri · Durga Puja, Children's Day and Karthika Masam. The VinaX team can switch any look on for everyone, so you may occasionally see one of these, or a festival outside its dates.

## If something looks wrong

| What you see | Why, and what to do |
|---|---|
| My accent colour is not the one I chose | A festival theme is on, or a preview is running. Your own colours return when it ends. |
| The festival look stays after the festival | A preview is still running. Choose **Stop preview**, or reload the app. |
| I closed the greeting card and want to see it again | Choose that festival in **Preview a festival**. |
