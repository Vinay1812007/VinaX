# Flow

This page covers Flow: a full-screen feed of songs that you swipe through. Each card plays a short stretch of one song, starting at a part worth hearing, so you can find something new quickly. The page explains how to open Flow, how to move through it, how to keep a song, and what happens to your own queue meanwhile.

## Open Flow

- On **Home** or **Discover**, find the **Flow** card and press **Start**.
- On a wide screen, **Flow** is also in the sidebar and in the command palette.

The phone tab bar does not have a Flow tab. Use the card.

To close Flow, press the close button at the top, or Esc on a keyboard.

## What a card does

- The card that settles on screen starts playing by itself.
- When the song has synced lyrics, the preview starts just before a line the song repeats, which is usually the chorus. Otherwise it starts about a third of the way in.
- A preview lasts 30 seconds. Then the feed moves to the next card.
- A thin line shows how much of the preview is left.
- When the song has synced lyrics, the line being sung shows on the card.
- A song that cannot play is passed over.

Flow leaves out songs you recently played, queued or skipped elsewhere in VinaX.

## Move through the feed

| To do this | Touch screen | Keyboard and mouse |
|---|---|---|
| Next song | Swipe up | Scroll, ↓, Page Down or j |
| Previous song | Swipe down | ↑, Page Up or k |
| Pause or play | Tap the card once | Space |
| Like the song | Double-tap the card | |
| Close Flow | Close button | Esc |

There are also previous and next buttons at the top, and a play/pause button on every card. A double-tap only ever likes a song; it never removes a like.

## Keep a song

Each card has these actions:

- **Like** adds the song to your Liked songs. Tap it again to remove the like.
- **Queue** adds the song to your own queue.
- **Play full song** plays the song from the start, placed next to what you were listening to before. After that, cards stop playing by themselves until you press play on one.
- **More like this** starts a radio from the song and opens the full-screen player.
- **Share** shares a link to the song, or copies it.
- The **⋮** menu is the same song menu as everywhere else.

## Your queue is safe

When you open Flow, VinaX sets your own queue aside. Previews do not add to it, do not go into your listening history, and do not change your recommendations. When you close Flow, your queue comes back, paused where you left it.

Songs that Flow shows you are remembered as shown, so other parts of the app are less likely to offer them again straight away.

For the queue itself, see [player and queue](player-and-queue.md).

## During Listen Together

If you are in a [Listen Together](listen-together.md) session, as host or guest, Flow does not change what is playing. It shows a notice with a link back to the session. You can still browse the cards and add songs to the queue.
