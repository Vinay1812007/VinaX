# Flow

Flow is a full-screen feed of songs to swipe through. It's for finding something new fast: each card plays a short stretch of one song, and you swipe on when it's not for you.

## Opening it

- **Home** and **Discover** each have a Flow card.
- On a computer, **Flow** is also in the sidebar and the command palette.
- On a phone, use the card on Home or Discover. The tab bar keeps its five tabs.

## What a card does

- The card that settles on screen starts playing by itself. It starts at the chorus when the song's synced lyrics show one, otherwise about a third of the way in.
- It plays about 30 seconds, then the feed moves to the next card.
- A thin line at the bottom shows how much of that stretch is left.
- When the song has synced lyrics, the line being sung shows on the card.

## Moving through the feed

| Phone | Computer |
|---|---|
| Swipe up for the next song, down for the one before | Scroll, ↓ / ↑, Page Down / Page Up, or j / k |
| Tap once to pause or play | Space |
| Double-tap to like (it never un-likes) | |
| | Esc closes Flow |

There are also previous and next buttons at the top, and a Pause / Play button on every card. A song that can't be played is skipped.

## Keeping a song

- **Like** adds it to Liked songs.
- **Queue** adds it to your real queue.
- **Play full song** adds it after what you were listening to and plays it from the start. After that, cards stop playing by themselves until you press Play on one.
- **More like this** starts AI Radio from the song and opens the full-screen player.
- **Share** and the **⋮** menu work as they do everywhere else.

## Your queue is safe

Opening Flow sets your own queue aside: the songs, where you were, and whether radio was on. Previews never add to it, never count as plays or skips, and never change your listening history. When you close Flow, your queue comes back, paused where you left it. Reloading the page while in Flow still finds your queue.

A song Flow shows you counts as "shown" for the rest of the app, so Home and the DJ are less likely to repeat it straight away.

## During Listen Together

If you're in a Listen Together session, as host or guest, Flow doesn't take over the music. It shows a short notice instead.
