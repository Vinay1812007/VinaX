# Friends and sharing

This page covers what VinaX 15 added for listening with other people and for finding new music every day: your public profile, following friends, Blend, shared playlists, Listen Together's chat and vote-to-skip, sharing a VinaX AI chat, Discover 5, release alerts, weekly goals and your year as a story. Everything here is off until you switch it on, and works on the web and the Android app.

## Your public profile

**Settings → Profile → Public profile.** Pick a handle (3–20 letters, digits or underscores) and, if you like, a line about yourself, then press **Create profile**. Your page is at `/u/<handle>`.

- **What it shows:** your Google name and picture, your line, your top artists and languages, and your most recent likes. VinaX works these out on your device and sends a short summary every half hour while the app is open.
- **Visible to everyone** is on when you create it. Switch it off and only people you follow who also follow you can see it.
- **Share what I'm playing** shows friends the song you have on, for a few minutes after you play it. Off by default.
- **Remove my profile** deletes the page and every follow, both ways. Your library and account stay.

## Following and Blend

Open someone's profile (a link they sent, or `/u/<handle>`) and press **Follow**. People you follow who share what they are playing appear in **Friends are listening** at the top of Home; tap the song to play it.

**Blend** on a profile builds a mix from their recent likes and yours, on your device, and starts playing it. It does not change either library.

## Shared playlists

On any of your playlists, open **⋯ → Share…**.

- **Share as a public page** makes a page anyone with the link can open, play and save to their own library.
- **Invite friends to add songs** makes an invite link. Whoever opens it can search and add songs, or remove them — no account needed. Their name shows beside what they added.
- **Update the shared copy** sends your latest list. If a friend added songs in the meantime, their additions come into your playlist first.
- **Stop sharing** deletes the page; the links stop working. Your playlist stays.

## Listen Together

Rooms (see [Listen together](listen-together.md)) now show everyone's name to everyone in the room, have a short **Chat** (the last 40 lines), and a **⏭** button: the host skips; guests vote, and the song moves on once more than half the room has voted. **Save this session as a playlist** keeps every song the room played.

## Share a VinaX AI chat

In a chat, open **Export → Share as a link…**. The link opens a read-only copy of the conversation's text (no pictures or attached files) for 90 days. You need to be signed in to share.

## Discover 5

A card among your daily cards on Home: five songs you have not heard, one at a time. **Keep** saves it to Listen Later, **Skip** moves on, **Play** plays it. On a phone, swipe right to keep and left to skip. A new five arrives the next day.

## Songs like this, and why

**⋯ → Songs like this** on any song (and **Player tools → Songs like this** in the full-screen player) queues ten similar songs after the one playing. Every song VinaX picks now says why in **Up next** — "Same artist as …", "Because you played …".

## Release alerts

Save an artist (the **Save** button on their page) and VinaX checks their songs twice a day. New songs from this year appear in **New from your artists** on Home, and on Android as a notification. A release shows once it has picked up some plays, so it can be a few hours behind the day it came out.

## Weekly goals and your year as a story

**Settings → Playback → Your goal** now also has **Minutes a week** and **New artists a week**. Progress and your streak of weeks met are on **Your VinaX** (the Stats page).

**Your year in music → Play as a story** plays your recap as full-screen slides: tap the right side for the next, the left side for the previous. The last slide shares the card.

## More in 15

- **Focus sounds** — Player tools → Focus sounds: rain, wind, a deep hum or an even hush beside the music, with a timer. The sleep timer stops them too.
- **Match the song** — Settings → Sound: the equaliser preset follows what is playing. Your own preset comes back when you switch it off.
- **Drive Mode voice** — the mic button: "play Srivalli", "next", "pause", "play something in Telugu".
- **Import a playlist from a file** — Library → Import, then **Open a file**: CSV exports from other apps, M3U playlists or plain text.
- **Smart downloads** (Android) — Settings → Playback: keeps this week's Weekly Mix downloaded, on Wi-Fi only.
- **VinaX AI attachments** — Word, Excel, PowerPoint and EPUB files are read for their text, on your device.
