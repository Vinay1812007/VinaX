# The player and the queue

This page covers what happens after you tap a song: the mini player and Now Playing, how the DJ builds what comes next, AI Radio, the Smart Queue switch, queueing songs yourself, the Queue page, Tune this queue, Pin a mood, and the player's other tools (sleep timer, speed, looping a passage, marks, resume, history). For the recommendation settings see [Discovery modes](discovery-modes.md).

## The mini player and Now Playing

When a song is playing, a mini player stays on screen with play or pause, previous and next. Tap it to open Now Playing, the full-screen player. Press `Esc` or go back to leave it.

The mini player and Now Playing look different in each app style. In one style the mini player is a solid bar, in another a rounded capsule, in another a full-width bar with the progress line along its top edge, and the Now Playing backdrop changes too. The controls and their labels are the same in every style. See [App styles](app-styles.md).

## Tap a song: the DJ builds what follows

Tap any song, in a shelf, an album, a playlist or search results. With the default settings the song starts and the DJ adds the next five songs to follow it. As the queue runs low, the DJ adds five more.

- To see why a song was picked: open its menu → **Why this song?**
- To make playback follow the list you tapped, in order: turn off **Settings → Recommendations → AI → DJ builds every queue**, or turn off **Smart Queue** on the Queue page.
- To choose which languages a queue may use: **Settings → Recommendations → Languages → Queue languages**. See [Discovery modes](discovery-modes.md).

## Queue a song yourself

Open a song's menu with the ⋯ button on the song. With a mouse, a right-click on a song opens a shorter menu of quick actions for it.

- To play a song straight after the current one: open its menu → **Play next**.
- To add a song to the queue: open its menu → **Add to queue**.
- On a touch screen, swipe a song row to the right to add it to the queue. Swipe left to save it to Listen Later.

Songs you add yourself stay in the queue when the DJ rebuilds its own picks.

## AI Radio

AI Radio plays endless music from one starting point. Open it from **AI Radio** on Home, or from **Start AI Radio** on an empty Queue page.

| Start from | What you do |
|---|---|
| A description | Type a few words in the field at the top, or use one of the examples. |
| **Pick a mood** | Tap Melody, Romantic, Dance, Chill, Sad, Devotional, DJ remix, Folk, Beats, Classics or New. |
| **From a song or artist** | Tap one of the songs or artists listed. |

Every song menu also has **Start AI Radio**: that song starts and the DJ keeps adding songs that follow from it. An artist page has the same item in its menu.

## The Queue page

To open the queue: tap the **Queue** button in the mini player or in Now Playing.

The page has up to three groups: **Now playing**, **Your songs** (what you added) and **Up next**. Each upcoming song can carry a mark:

| Mark | What it means |
|---|---|
| **VinaX pick** | The app chose it to follow what is playing. **Refresh up next** replaces it. |
| **Added by you** | You added it. A rebuild does not move or remove it. |
| No mark | It came from the album, playlist or list you tapped. |

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| The handle on a row | Drag to reorder. With a keyboard, focus the handle and press `↑` or `↓`. |
| ⋯ on a row | **Keep this song**, **Move up**, **Move down**, **Clear from here down**, **Remove from queue**. |
| **Keep this song** | Turns a VinaX pick into one of your songs, so a rebuild leaves it alone. |
| **Refresh up next** | Asks the DJ for a fresh set. Songs you added stay. |
| Sort chips | Reorder the upcoming songs: **Energy**, **Calm first**, **Newest**, **Classics**, **Mood arc**. |
| **Save as playlist** | Saves the queue as a playlist you keep. |
| **Build a queue** | Plans a longer session for you to review and add. |

If you remove a song by mistake, tap **Undo** in the message that appears.

## Smart Queue

**Smart Queue** is a switch on the Queue page. The line next to it says what it is doing:

- On: "The next song you start leads, and the DJ builds the rest."
- Off: "Your songs play in the order you chose."

**More in Settings** opens the full recommendation settings.

## Tune this queue

Tune this queue is a row of chips that reshapes what plays next in one tap. Find it on the Queue page, or in Now Playing → **Player tools** → **Tune this queue**.

| Chips |
|---|
| More energetic · More chill · More romantic · More melody · More beats · Devotional · DJ remix · Folk · Heartbreak · More classics · More new · Same language · Switch language · Surprise me |

Tap a chip and the upcoming songs are retuned. Songs you added stay. The tune stays active until you start a fresh song.

## Pin a mood

To hold a mood for a while: open Now Playing → **Up next** tab → **Pin a mood** → tap **Romantic**, **Energetic**, **Chill**, **Melancholy** or **Devotional**.

A pin lasts 45 minutes. Tap the same chip again to unpin.

## Now Playing

| Gesture or control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Swipe the artwork left or right | Next or previous song |
| Double-tap the left or right edge of the artwork | Back or forward 10 seconds |
| Double-tap the centre of the artwork | Like the song |
| **Up next**, **Lyrics**, **Credits** tabs | The coming songs, the lyrics, or the song's credits |
| **Lyrics** button | Full-screen lyrics |
| **Queue** button | Opens the Queue page |
| **Player tools** button | Volume, Speed, Loop a passage, Marks, Tune this queue, and More |
| ⋯ menu | **Drive mode**, **Karaoke** (when the song has synced lyrics), **Listen together**, **Share now-playing card**, then the usual song actions |

Inside **Player tools**:

- **Speed** changes the playback speed.
- **Loop a passage** repeats the part of the song between two points you set.
- **Marks** saves a moment in the song so you can jump back to it.
- **Player tools → Share this moment** shares or copies a link that opens the song at the current position.
- **Player tools → Ambient mode** is a switch (the pill reads "Ambient mode on" or "off"). When it is on and you leave the player untouched for 45 seconds while music plays, the screen changes to artwork and a clock.

## Sleep timer

To stop the music later: open the **Sleep timer** and choose **15m**, **30m**, **60m** or **End of song**, or under **Sleep after songs** choose **3**, **5** or **10**.

With a timed setting, the last 30 seconds fade out. To stop a running timer, tap **Cancel**.

## Resume where you left off

When you return to a song you left part-way, VinaX starts it where you stopped and shows "Resumed from" with the time.

- This applies to songs longer than 60 seconds, when you stopped after the first 20 seconds and before the last 20 seconds.
- Positions for your last 80 songs are kept, on this device only.
- To turn it off: **Settings → Playback → Resume playback**.

## History

To see what you played: open **Library** → **History**.

- Search by title or artist, or filter by date (**Today**, **Yesterday**, **This week**, **Last 30 days**) and by language.
- To clear part of it, use **Clear last hour**, **Clear today** or **Clear all history**.

History holds your last 150 plays and stays on this device. For your saved music and backups, see [Library and backup](library-and-backup.md).
