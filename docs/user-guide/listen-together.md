# Listen Together

This page covers listening with friends at the same moment: starting a session, joining one, staying in the session while you use the rest of the app, adding songs, reacting, and leaving. It works in the web app and the Android app. A guest needs no sign-up and no app; a browser is enough.

## Open Listen Together

Use any of these:

- **Library → Listen Together**.
- The menu in the full-screen player → **Listen together**.
- **Listen Together** in the command palette on a computer (see [keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.md)).
- The **Listen Together** tile on Home's first-visit welcome.

## Host a session

1. Under **Host a session**, press **Start a session**.
2. You get a 6-character room code, a QR code and an invite link.
3. Press **Share invite** to send the link (where your device cannot share, the link is copied instead). Friends can also scan the QR code, or you can press **Copy code** and send the code.
4. Play music as usual, from any page. Everyone in the room hears what you play.

You stay in control: play, pause, seek and skip as usual. The room page shows the song everyone hears, **Up next**, and how many people are listening. Only the host sees listeners' names. Guests see the count.

## Join a session

- **With a link:** open the invite link. It joins by itself.
- **With a code:** open Listen Together, type the code under **Join a session** and press **Join**.

Your player then follows the host: the same song at the same position. If your player drifts by more than about a second, it catches up by itself. While you follow, VinaX does not add songs of its own to your queue.

The room page tells you how it is going: **Live · in sync**, **Live · syncing…** or **Live · catching up…**.

### Tap to start listening

A browser does not let a page start sound until you have tapped it. This happens, for example, when you open an invite link and touch nothing. When the host is playing and your player is silent, VinaX shows **Tap to start listening**. Tap it once and your player starts at the host's position.

## Stay in the session on other pages

A session keeps running while you use the rest of VinaX. A host can go to Search for the next song; a guest can open the lyrics. It also survives a reload of the tab.

On every page except the room itself, a small pill shows the session and its room code:

| The pill says | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Hosting · 3 listening | You are the host, with the number of people in the room |
| Listening with Asha | You are a guest and in step with the host |
| Asha went quiet | The host's device has sent nothing for a minute and a half. Your music keeps playing. |
| Reconnecting… | The connection dropped and is coming back |
| Tap to start listening | Your browser is waiting for a tap before it plays |

Tap the pill to go back to the room.

## Add songs and react

- **Guests:** press **Add a song for everyone** on the room page and search for a song. It is sent to the host's app, which adds it to the shared queue. Songs added by a guest show **Added by** and the guest's name under **Up next**.
- **The host:** press **Add to the queue** on the same page, or queue songs anywhere in the app as usual. If a guest's song is not available, the host is told.
- **Reactions:** tap an emoji under **React to the music**. It appears on the screens in the room.

## Leave or end

- A guest presses **Leave session**. Only that guest leaves; the room carries on.
- The host presses **End for everyone**. Guests are told that the host ended the session.
- A guest who closes the tab leaves the room.
- A host who closes the tab without ending the session leaves the room open. Guests see that the host went quiet. Within a day, the host can type the room code under **Join a session** on the same device to carry on as host.

## Flow during a session

While you are in a session, [Flow](flow.md) does not change what is playing. You can still browse it and add songs to the queue.

## What others can see

Guests see how many people are listening, not who they are. The host sees the names. Songs a guest adds carry that guest's name. Your library, history and taste are not part of a session. The full list of what a session shares is in [data and privacy](../data-and-privacy.md).
