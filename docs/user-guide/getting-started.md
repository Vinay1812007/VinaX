# Getting started

This page covers the first five minutes: the welcome screen, where things are, and how to play a first song. It applies to the web app and the Android app.

## The welcome screen

On first open VinaX asks for three things.

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| Name | At least two characters. Used to greet you. |
| Username | 3–20 lowercase letters, numbers or `_`. VinaX suggests one from your name and checks that it is free while you type. If you are offline it is confirmed later. |
| Languages | The languages you listen in. These become your pinned languages; you can change them in Settings. |

A checkbox, unticked by default, asks whether you want to share anonymous usage statistics. You can change your mind at any time in Settings → Region & Privacy → Share anonymous usage.

There is no password and no login. If you already use VinaX on another device, the same screen has **Move from old device** (scan a QR or type a code) and **Import a file** (restore a backup). See [Library and backup](library-and-backup.md).

After that, VinaX may show a grid of trending songs in your first language. Tap the ones you like; they become favourites and give Home something to work with. You can skip this step.

Then a short tour runs. **Skip** closes it. The last slide offers **Live walkthrough**, which starts the first guided tutorial. Help → **Replay the welcome tour** shows the tour again.

## Where things are

There are five destinations: **Home**, **Discover**, **Search**, **Library** and **VinaX AI**. On a phone they are the dock at the bottom of the screen. On wider screens they are the sidebar.

The top bar shows where you are and the actions for that page. On Home those actions are the theme toggle and the notification bell. The top bar also links to Settings. It has no search box: Search is its own destination. On a keyboard, `Ctrl/⌘ + K` opens the command palette from anywhere.

Discover and Library each open with a grid of shortcuts to their other pages:

| Page | Shortcuts |
|---|---|
| Discover | Charts, Languages, Moods, Regions, Movies, Videos, Made For You, Your Week, AI Playlist, Ads |
| Library | Favorites, Listen Later, Downloads, History, Your VinaX, Taste Profile |

## Play a first song

- On Home, press the play button on the **Aura Mix** card. The mix is built from your pinned languages and your listening on this device.
- Or tap **AI Radio**, the first tile in Home's shortcut row, and start endless music from a mood, an artist or a few words. See [The player and the queue](player-and-queue.md#ai-radio).
- Or tap any song anywhere. The song starts, and the DJ builds the next five songs from it, led by that song's language and starting with familiar songs. See [The player and the queue](player-and-queue.md).
- Tap the heart to save a song to your favourites. Likes, skips and finished songs shape your taste profile, which stays on this device.

Home also has **Jump back in** (recently played), **Your playlists** (the ones you made, then the ones you saved), **Popular picks for you** (popular songs in the order your taste suggests), **Your top genres** once you have liked or played enough of one genre, and quick chips such as **Trending**, **Charts** and **Lyrics** (Lyrics appears while a song is loaded in the player).

Until you arrange Home yourself, it arranges itself: the sections you use most, the time of day, whether you finish or skip new discoveries, and the genres you like decide the order. It is worked out when you open the app and never moves while you are looking at it. **Customise Home**, near the end of the page, reorders or hides sections; an order you set there is kept exactly.

## Live tutorials

Help lists five guided walkthroughs that run inside the real app and highlight the actual controls: *Play your first song*, *Find any song*, *Ask VinaX AI*, *Make it yours* and *Save and organise*. The first one starts a real song. Leave any tutorial with `Esc` or **Skip**.

## Keep your music safe

Your library and settings live on this device. Export a backup from **Settings → Your Data** before clearing browser data or reinstalling. See [Library and backup](library-and-backup.md).
