# Getting started

This page covers the first five minutes: the welcome screen, where things are, how to play a first song, and how VinaX looks and talks to you (themes, accents, frosted glass, snackbars and notifications). It applies to the web app and the Android app.

VinaX is free: no subscription, no premium tier, no email and no password. On the website one labelled advertisement sits at the end of song, artist, album, language and mood pages; there are never ads in the player, the queue, VinaX AI, your library, the Android app or Kid mode.

## The welcome screen

On first open the welcome sheet says it first — **Free. No sign-up.** — and then asks for three things.

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| Name | At least two characters. Used to greet you. |
| Username | 3–20 lowercase letters, numbers or `_`. VinaX suggests one from your name and checks that it is free while you type. If you are offline it is confirmed later. |
| Languages | The languages you listen in. These become your pinned languages; you can change them in Settings. |

A checkbox, unticked by default, asks whether you want to share anonymous usage statistics. You can change your mind at any time in Settings → Region & Privacy → Share anonymous usage.

There is no password and no login. If you already use VinaX on another device, the same screen has **Move from old device** (scan a QR or type a code) and **Import a file** (restore a backup). See [Library and backup](library-and-backup.md).

After that, VinaX may show a grid of trending songs in your first language. Tap the ones you like; they become favourites and give Home something to work with. You can skip this step.

Then a short tour of eight slides runs: the five places to go, Home, tapping a song, steering what plays next, finding music, VinaX AI, Listen Together and keeping your library. **Skip** closes it. The last slide offers **Live walkthrough**, which starts the first guided tutorial. Help → **Replay the welcome tour** shows the tour again.

## Where things are

There are five destinations: **Home**, **Discover**, **Search**, **Library** and **VinaX AI**. On a phone they are the tab bar at the bottom of the screen. On wider screens they are the sidebar, with your library in its own panel below the others.

The top bar shows where you are and the actions for that page; it turns to frosted glass once the page scrolls under it. On Home those actions are the theme toggle and the notification bell. The top bar also links to Settings. It has no search box: Search is its own destination. On a keyboard, `Ctrl/⌘ + K` opens the command palette from anywhere.

Discover and Library each open with a grid of shortcuts to their other pages:

| Page | Shortcuts |
|---|---|
| Discover | Charts, Languages and Moods first, then Regions, Movies, Videos, Made for you, Your week and AI playlist |
| Library | Favorites, Listen Later, Downloads, History, Your VinaX, Taste Profile |

## Play a first song

On your very first visit, before anything has played on this device, Home opens with a welcome: **All the music you love. Free.**, the languages VinaX plays, and two buttons — **Start listening**, which plays the opening mix at once, and **Pick your languages**. Below it, **Everything included, all free** has a tile each for VinaX AI, AI Radio, Listen Together, synced lyrics, offline downloads on Android and the language pages. Once you have played something, Home greets you by name instead.

- On Home, press the play button on the **Aura Mix** card. The mix is built from your pinned languages and your listening on this device.
- Or tap **AI Radio**, the first tile in Home's shortcut row, and start endless music from a mood, an artist or a few words. See [The player and the queue](player-and-queue.md#ai-radio).
- Or tap any song anywhere. The song starts, and the DJ builds the next five songs from it, led by that song's language and starting with familiar songs. See [The player and the queue](player-and-queue.md).
- Tap the heart to save a song to your favourites. Likes, skips and finished songs shape your taste profile, which stays on this device.

Home opens with the **Aura Mix** and its big play button, **AI Radio** and **Surprise me** beside it, and **Jump back in** (recently played). Below come the sections that are yours: **Made for you** (your mixes, one card each), **Continue listening**, **Because you liked …**, **Your playlists**, **For you this week**, your favourite artists and **Similar artists**. **More from your listening** opens On this day, On repeat, Most listened and the rewinds. **Fresh discoveries** shows what is popular near you and in your languages (**Popular picks for you** is popular songs in the order your taste suggests); **More trending** opens the rest.

Under **Explore more** are chips for your languages, Charts, Trending, Moods, Regions and Lyrics (while a song is loaded), **Refresh Home**, and **Show more for you**, which opens the wider catalogue: charts, moods, **Your top genres** once you have liked or played enough of one genre, trending artists and albums, picks for the time of day, recently liked and an endless feed. VinaX remembers what you opened until you close the app.

Home keeps its shelves while you come and go: it builds them again when you pull down or tap **Refresh Home**, and on its own when you come back after half an hour. A song you hide, a language you mute or Kid mode leaves every shelf at once.

Until you arrange Home yourself, it arranges itself: the sections you use most, the time of day, whether you finish or skip new discoveries, and the genres you like decide the order. It is worked out when you open the app and never moves while you are looking at it. **Customise Home**, near the end of the page, reorders or hides sections; an order you set there is kept exactly.

## Live tutorials

Help lists six guided walkthroughs that run inside the real app and highlight the actual controls: *Play your first song*, *Find any song* (suggestions as you type and the Top result), *Listen Together*, *Ask VinaX AI* (connectors and the tool timeline), *Make it yours* (accents, frosted glass, sound) and *Save and organise*. The first one starts a real song. Leave any tutorial with `Esc` or **Skip**.

## How VinaX looks

- **Themes.** Settings → Appearance → Theme: Dark (warm plum, the default), Black, Light (warm cream), System, or Auto (light by day, dark at night).
- **Accent.** **Marigold** is the default. Copper, Sunset, Gold, Emerald, Ocean, Azure, Violet, Rose and Mono sit beside it, and Custom accent takes any colour; VinaX builds a readable palette from it.
- **Frosted glass.** The top bar, the tab bar, the player, menus and sheets are frosted panes that let the page show through softly. **Glass effect** and **Background blur** in Settings set how see-through and how hazy they are. The Black theme, and a device set to reduce transparency, keep them solid.
- **Now Playing.** The full-screen player sits on a slow drift of your song's artwork, heavily blurred. It holds still when reduced motion is on.
- **Festivals.** On festival days the app takes on a festive look; Settings → Festival themes keeps one look all year.

## Snackbars and notifications

When you like a song, queue one or undo something, a small message — a snackbar — rises above the player: the song's cover, one line and at most one button, such as **View** (open Liked songs or the queue) or **Undo**. At most two show at once and each leaves after about four seconds. Hover over it or focus it to keep it; swipe it down or press `Esc` to dismiss it.

The bell in Home's top bar opens **Notifications**: announcements and song picks grouped by day (Today, Yesterday, then the date), then **From VinaX** with the latest release notes. In the Android app you can mute alerts for seven days from there.

## Keep your music safe

Your library and settings live on this device. Export a backup from **Settings → Your Data** before clearing browser data or reinstalling. See [Library and backup](library-and-backup.md).
